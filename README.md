# Hi, I'm Carsten 👋

Solo builder from Germany. I help small businesses — mostly in the trades — put AI to work, and I build and run the systems myself, in production, every day.

Most of it is built together with Claude Code: apps vibe-coded from idea to production, and autonomous agents that do their work in the background without anyone clicking a button.

## Products

- **AAIOS** *(private)* — my AI operating system and the backbone of everything else: a social planner publishing to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok and YouTube, and **Javis**, a voice-enabled AI coworker with tool use, long-term memory, a browser agent and an approve-before-write gate. Exposed to other AIs via an MCP server.
- **AI-Tresor** — local approval vault for AI agents (Go · Wails · macOS/Windows). Agents ask for access, a human approves; the secret never appears in chat. → [ai-tresor.com](https://ai-tresor.com)

## JARVIS — operations app for a pipe & sewer company *(private)*

A standalone web app that runs the day-to-day of a German pipe and sewer services company (drain cleaning, rehabilitation, CCTV inspection) in one interface, with **JARVIS** as the built-in AI assistant:

- jobs, customers, contacts and sites, scheduling with suggested appointments
- email, calendar, invoices (accounting integration), phone calls and voice notes
- inspection videos, documents and a knowledge base in the same place
- role-based logins for owner, management and office, plus an audit log of every external write

FastAPI · SQLModel · React · TypeScript · Tailwind

## Autonomous background agents (AAIOS)

About a dozen scheduled jobs running unattended: incoming invoices from the mailbox into accounting, review requests a few days after a job, emergency calls turned into leads and alerts, competitor and website monitoring, lead sync and weekly AI reports.
